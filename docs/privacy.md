# Privacy policy: AI Forecast Factory

_Last updated 17 October 2026_

AI Forecast Factory does not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**Forecasts are computed on your device.** The weather model runs entirely on your iPhone
or iPad; nothing about your forecasts leaves it.

**Your location.** Only if you choose "Use my current location" in Places: your location is
used on the device to find the nearest model grid point, and sent to Apple's MapKit service
to look up the place's name. It is kept only as that saved place, on your device.

**Place search.** What you type when adding a place is sent to Apple's MapKit service to
find matching places. Apple's privacy policy applies to that service.

**Downloads.** The app downloads weather data from the European Centre for Medium-Range
Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) and its mirrors on Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure, and the
forecast model from Hugging Face. These requests carry nothing about you beyond what any
web request does, such as your IP address, which those services handle under their own
privacy policies.

**What stays on your device.** Saved places, settings, downloaded data and forecasts are
kept in the app's own storage and are removed when you delete the app.

**Contact.** Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/amarchakitus/AIFS-iOS-app/issues.
